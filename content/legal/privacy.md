# Privacy Notice

**Version** Draft 0.2. **Effective** not in effect (draft for attorney review).

This notice explains what personal information Bluegrass Advisory Group, LLC ("BAG") collects through the Site and what we do with it.

### 1. What we collect

- **What you give us:** name, company, job title, email, phone, and what you write in a form or say when booking a call. If you use a free demo tool on the Site, what you type into it.
- **Booking details:** the time you pick and anything you enter on the booking page.
- **Automatic data:** The Site runs Google Analytics 4 on every page with its standard settings. It uses cookies to record the pages you view and technical details of your visit, such as browser and device type and an approximate location based on your IP address. When you send the contact form, we also store your IP address and browser type with your submission. The Site uses no other analytics tool and no advertising pixels.

### 2. How we use it

To reply to you, schedule and prepare for a call, scope and quote a possible engagement, keep a record of what you agreed to, run and secure the Site, and meet legal duties. If you opt in, we may send you occasional email about our services; every email has an unsubscribe link.

### 3. AI tools

We may use AI tools, under business terms that do not allow the provider to train on our data, to summarize your intake answers and draft our reply. A person at BAG reviews anything we send you.

### 4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only with:
- service providers that run the Site and our business for us (hosting, forms, scheduling, email, and our client records system): Vercel (hosts the Site), Supabase (stores contact form submissions), Airtable (our client records system, which receives contact form submissions and call bookings), Resend (sends the form notification and confirmation emails), Cal.com (the booking page for calls), Google (Google Analytics, described in section 1), and Anthropic (produces the results of the free demo tools from what you type into them).;
- people who work with us on an engagement, under confidentiality terms; and
- authorities when the law requires, or to protect our rights, or a buyer if we sell the business.

### 5. How long we keep it

Intake and booking information for prospects who do not become clients: 24 months after last contact. Client records follow the client's contract.

### 6. Your choices

You may ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending marketing email, by writing to phil@bluegrassadvisorygroup.com. We will answer within 45 days. We will not treat you differently for asking.

### 7. Do Not Track and other tracking

The Site does not respond to Do Not Track signals. Google Analytics runs whether or not your browser sends one.

### 8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, but no website or email system is completely secure.

### 9. Children

The Site is for businesses. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

### 10. Changes and contact

We will post changes here with a new version number and effective date. Questions: phil@bluegrassadvisorygroup.com, Lexington, Kentucky.
